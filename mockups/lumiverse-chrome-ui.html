VELLUM × Lumiverse — Themed Drawer Tabs & Input Area

The real Lumiverse drawer rail (56 px sidebar + peeking panel) and floating composer pill, reconstructed from ViewportDrawer.module.css and InputArea.module.css, then re-skinned by the eight new Vellum chromes. Each block only remaps the app's own --lumiverse-* variables (primary / bg-deep / border / fill / text) plus a couple of signature chrome accents — exactly how a .lumitheme pack restyles the app. Active tab shows the inset 3px primary rail; composer shows the :focus-within ring and themed send button.
