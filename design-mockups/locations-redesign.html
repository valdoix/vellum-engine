The Estate Map

Containment made literal: each place that holds others is a framed enclosure, and the places inside it sit within its border. You read nesting as physical enclosure, not indentation. Regions frame in cool blue; places in gold. The scale tag (region / site / room) labels depth.

The Gazetteer

A cartographer's index rather than a tree. Regions become quiet section headers with a fading rule; places list beneath, contained places one indent deeper with a hairline elbow. Dense, scannable, documentary — the current place carries a gold spine. Controls stay hidden until hover so the column reads clean.
