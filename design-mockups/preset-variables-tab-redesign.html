Concept I

The Loom

Variables are warp threads on a manuscript loom. Each block is a spool; its variables hang beneath on a gilt thread. Warm, editorial, unmistakably VELLUM — the primary content is the variable grid, with diagnostics tucked into a hairline drawer at the foot.

Concept II

Control Desk

The engine-room read. Variables become instrument modules — sliders render as bar gauges, selects as LED banks, switches as console toggles. A stat strip up top surfaces token budget and link/health at a glance. For the power user who thinks of a preset as a machine to tune.

Concept III

Marginalia

The tab reads as an illuminated page. A drop-cap crowns it; each variable is a margin gloss — an initial, an italic name, and an inline editable value underlined like an annotator's ink. The most beautiful, most restrained option: it hides the machinery and lets the values feel hand-set.
