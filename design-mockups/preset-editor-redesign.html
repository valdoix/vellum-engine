Point of View
Whose eyes the story looks through. Watch the manuscript above rewrite as you choose.

Second person — “you”; the reader is the character
Third limited — perceive only what the focal character could

Concept III

The Engine Room

For power users. The preset laid bare as an instrument panel — every block a labelled system with its raw variable path exposed, a live token meter, and a sidebar of subsystems. Cold, precise, diagnostic. This is the cockpit view.

Concept IV

The Ritual Interface

The boldest departure. The preset as an orrery — a summoning circle where the companion sits at the core and every subsystem orbits it as a glyph-node you can tap to wake or dim. Radial, ceremonial, and unlike any settings panel you have seen. Hover a node to bring it forward.
